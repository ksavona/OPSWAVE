# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OpsWeave should be a welcoming, professional, and constructive project. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and respect;
- give specific, evidence-based feedback;
- accept reasonable disagreement without personal attacks;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive information; and
- focus discussion on improving the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For urgent or sensitive conduct concerns, use the repository's private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify the report as a conduct matter. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this code. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical.
